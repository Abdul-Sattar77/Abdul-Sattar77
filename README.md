## About

I'm a software engineer who builds **AI-powered products end to end** — the model, the backend, the interface, and the deployment that makes it real.

Most of my work lives at the point where machine learning stops being a notebook experiment and starts being something a person can actually use: a farmer pointing a phone at a diseased leaf, a sales team working a pipeline that filled itself, a brief that comes back as a finished video. I care about the whole path from idea to shipped product, not just the interesting middle.

I co-founded **[ClusterCrew](https://github.com/Abdul-Sattar77/clustercrew-website)**, an AI and digital services studio, where I build the AI and web products we ship.

## How I Work

**Start from the problem, not the framework.** &nbsp;The stack is a consequence of what the thing has to do, not the other way round.

**Use AI where it changes the outcome.** &nbsp;A model earns its place when it does something ordinary code can't. Everywhere else, ordinary code is faster, cheaper and easier to reason about.

**A model in a notebook is not a product.** &nbsp;Training is the easy half. The work is getting it into something with a UI, auth, error states and a deploy target.

**Design for portability early.** &nbsp;Typed boundaries, validated inputs, schemas that don't need rewriting to move from a laptop to production.

**Write for the next person reading it.** &nbsp;Usually that person is me, six months later, with no memory of why any of it made sense.
